3. How to add printers?


Easy Steps:

 

1. Click Files->Printers

2. Click New button

3. Locate PrinterName

     In the Windows taskbar, search for Printers & Scanners and open it.

     Browse through the list of printers connected to your network and select the one you want to add.

4. Fill in the Printer Details

     Area - Enter the location of the printer (e.g., "Office 1" or "Reception").

     PrinterName - Enter the exact name of the printer as listed in your computer.

5. Click Save
