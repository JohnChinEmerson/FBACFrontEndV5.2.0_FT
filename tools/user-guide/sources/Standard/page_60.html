Customers


A. Click Files->Customers

    • View - to view customers
    • New - to add new customer
    • Print - to print customers

 

B. How to add/edit/delete new customer?

    Easy Steps:

1. Click Files->Customers

2. Click New button

3. Fill-in textboxes

   Customer - customer name / business name

   ContactNo - customer contact number

   Address  - customer address

   Customer TIN - customer tax identification number

   TermDays - customer grace period on days before payment for charge sales transactions

   Price Category - customer item price category

   HasRebate - yes if customer has loyalty points

   Discount% - customer discount percent

   DiscountID - customer discount identification number

   BusinessStyle - customer business style (i.e. trading, general merchandise etc)

4. Click Save

...to edit/delete
