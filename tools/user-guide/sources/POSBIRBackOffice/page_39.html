21. How to view sales report?


Easy Steps:

 

1. Click Sales->Sales Report

2. Select tab

    • Yearly
    • Monthly
    • Daily
    • Void Sales
    • Discount Sales
    • Customer Sales
    • WHTax Sales
    • VAT Sales
    • Sales Rebate Paid

3. Select Filter Criteria-> Click Go
