End-of-Day Inventory


    A. Click Menu ->End-of-Day Inventory

    • View - to view end-of-day inventory for specific day 
    • Ingredients Used- to inventory the ingredients in specific day
    • Print - to print end-of-day inventory for specific day

    B. How inventory ingredients in specific day?

        Easy Steps:

1. Select Date .

2. Click Ingredients Used  to display all ingredients used that day.

3. Input the actual count of each ingredient in physical count.  

4. Click Save.
