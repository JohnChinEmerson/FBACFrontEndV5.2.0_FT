29. How to extract reports for filing taxes?

 
 
    • The filing of taxes depends on the date indicated in BIR Form 2303, or the Certificate of Registration, for each business.
    • Each business has different tax filing compliance requirements.
    • The bookkeeper or accountant for each business is mostly responsible for filing.

        SALES:

           1. Go to Sales Reports->VAT Sales

           2. Select Date Range->Go

           3. Click Print or Export to Excel

 

           SALES DISCOUNTS:

           1. Go to Sales Reports->Discount Sales

           2. Select Date Range->Go

           3. Click Print or Export to Excel


           PURCHASES:

           1. Go to Receiving->Receiving Receipts

           2. Select DateRange->Click Go

           3. Click Print or Export to Excel

           EXPENSES:

           1. Go to Expenses->Expense Receipts

           2. Select DateRange->Click Go

           3. Click Print or Export to Excel

        
          VAT INPUT TAX:

           1. Go to Accounting->General Ledger

           2. Select VAT Recoverables

           3. Select DateRange->Click Go

           4. Click Print or Export to Excel


          VAT OUTPUT TAX:

           1. Go to Accounting->General Ledger

           2. Select VAT Payables

           3. Select DateRange->Click Go

           4. Click Print or Export to Excel
