Inventory Config

      A. Click Inventory->Inventory Config

    • (Inventory Stocks) Report Positive Variance As
      • Adjustment - the qty will appear as variance
      • Payable - the qty will appear as variance and amount will appear on payables
    • (Inventory Stocks) Report Negative Variance As
      • Adjustment - the qty will appear as variance
      • Expense - the qty will appear as variance and amount will appear as expense
      • Receivable - the qty will appear as variance and amount will appear on receivables
