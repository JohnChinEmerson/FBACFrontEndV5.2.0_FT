Register the POS to the BIR


Easy Steps:

 

  1. Upon request of the software agents from their clients, Mariosoft Solutions will provide Google Forms to be completed by the software users (clients).
  2. Upon receiving a response from Google Forms, Mariosoft Solutions will use the details for BIR registration, which will take 1 to 3 days to complete.
  3. After the registration process, Mariosoft Solutions will provide documents to the software users (clients) such as the BIR confirmation email with transaction number, Application for Registration for Sales Machines, and Sworn Declaration.
  4. Software users (clients) will submit the documents to BIR provided by Mariosoft Solutions, and approval will take 2-3 days.
  5. Once approved, Mariosoft Solutions will receive a notification email from the BIR and will issue a Permit to Use and decals to the software users (clients).
  6. After the issuance of the PTU and decals, technical support from Mariosoft Solutions will check the system to clear up transactions and set up the approved Permit to Use.
