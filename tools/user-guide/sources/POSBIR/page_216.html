Submit ESales to BIR


          Easy Steps:

  1. Click BIR->Z-Receipts
  2. Select Terminal->Month->Go
  3. Click Export to Excel Icon

 

  1. Click BIR->E-Receipts
  2. Select Terminal->Month->Go
  3. Click Export to Excel Icon

 

  1. Click BIR->E-Sales Book
  2. Select Terminal->Month->Go
  3. Click Export to Excel Icon

 

Upload the exported excel file to your BIR ESales Account

  1. To Access Esales account go to www.bir.gov.ph
  2. Click EServices
  3. Click Other services
  4. Click ESales
  5. Enroll Authorized user if you do not have the ESales Account yet
