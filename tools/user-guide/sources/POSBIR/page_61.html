Suppliers


A. Click Files->Suppliers

    • View - to view suppliers
    • New - to add new supplier
    • Print - to print suppliers

 

B. How to add/edit/delete new supplier?

    Easy Steps:

1. Click Files->Suppliers

2. Click New button

3. Fill-in textboxes

   SupplierName - supplier name / business name

   ContactPerson - supplier contact person

   Contact# - supplier contact number

   Address  - supplier address

   Supplier TIN - supplier tax identification number

   TermDays - supplier grace period on days before payment for charge receiving transactions

   BusinessStyle - supplier business style (i.e. trading, general merchandise etc)

4. Click Save

...to edit/delete
