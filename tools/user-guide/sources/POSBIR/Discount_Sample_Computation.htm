BIR Sample Computation

Discount Types

  • Senior - Senior Citizen Discount (20% or as per BIR memorandum order)
  • PWD - Persons with Disability Discount (20% or BIR memorandum order)
  • Solo Parent - Solo Parent Discount (10% or BIR memorandum order)
  • Regular - Regular Customer Discount (set by company policy, e.g., 5% -10%)
  • NAAC - National Athletes and Coaches Discount (20% or BIR memorandum order)
  • MOV - Medal of Valor Awardee Discount (20% or BIR memorandum order)


Scenario Structure

For each scenario, document the following:

  1. Sale Total Amount - Total sales or receipts for the transaction.
  2. Pax Count - Number of customers involved in the transaction.
  3. Discount Name - Discount category (e.g., Senior, PWD, Solo Parent, Regular, NAAC, MOV).
  4. Discount Person - Name of the person receiving the discount.
  5. Discount TIN - TIN number of the discounted person (if applicable).
  6. Discount ID - Valid ID number of the discounted person.
  7. Discount Count - Number of persons receiving the discount.
  8. Discount Value - Discount percentage based on law (for Senior, PWD, Solo Parent, NAAC, MOV) or company policy (for Regular).
  9. Discount Amount - Expected total discount amount applied to the sale.

Scenario 2: Single Senior Customer (applicable also for PWD)

  • Description: One customer qualifies for Senior discount.
  • Pax Count: 1
  • Discount Type(s): Senior or PWD
  • Discount Basis: 20% discount on eligible items, VAT exempt

Calculation Steps

  1. Start with Total Sales:
    ?150.00
  2. Divide by Pax Count to get per-person share:
    ?150.00 ÷ 1 pax = ?150.00 per person
  3. Determine total amount for discounted persons:
    Discounted Persons Count = 1
    ?150.00 × 1 discounted person = ?150.00 (eligible for discount)
  4. Remove VAT (12%) for VAT-exempt Senior:
    ?150.00 ÷ 1.12 = ?133.93 (VAT Exempt Sales)
    Less VAT = ?150.00 ? ?133.93 = ?16.07
  5. Apply 20% Senior Discount or PWD:
    ?133.93 × 20% = ?26.79
  6. Compute Total Due:
    ?133.93 ? ?26.79 = ?107.14
  7. VAT-Breakdown on the receipt:
    VATable Sales: 0.00

VATAmount: 0.00

Scenario 5: Mixed Discounts - Senior and Regular

Description: Group of 5 customers: 1 Senior, 1 Regular (5% discount), and 3 with no discount.

Pax Count: 5
Discount Type(s): Senior (20% discount, VAT exempt) + Regular (5% discount, company policy)
Discount Basis:

  • Senior - 20% discount on eligible items, VAT exempt
  • Regular - 5% discount on eligible items, not VAT exempt

Calculation Steps

  1. Start with Total Sales:
    ?1,000.00
  2. Divide by Pax Count to get per-person share:
    ?1,000.00 ÷ 5 pax = ?200.00 per person
  3. Determine total amount for each discounted person:
    • Senior: ?200.00
    • Regular: ?200.00

Senior Discount Calculation (VAT Exempt)

  1. Remove VAT (12%) from Senior's share:
    ?200.00 ÷ 1.12 = ?178.57 (VAT Exempt Sales)
    Less VAT = ?200.00 ? ?178.57 = ?21.43
  2. Apply 20% Senior Discount:
    ?178.57 × 20% = ?35.71
  3. Senior Total After Discount:
    ?178.57 ? ?35.71 = ?142.86

Regular Discount Calculation (Company Policy)

  1. Apply 5% Regular Discount (no VAT exemption):
    ?200.00 × 5% = ?10.00
  2. Regular Total After Discount:
    ?200.00 ? ?10.00 = ?190.00

Non-Discounted Customers (3 pax)

  1. Total for 3 regular-paying pax:
    3 × ?200.00 = ?600.00

Final Total Due Calculation

  1. Add all amounts:
    Senior: ?142.86
    Regular: ?190.00
    Non-discounted pax: ?600.00

Total Due = ?142.86 + ?190.00 + ?600.00 = ?932.86
