21. How to transact canvass-purchase order-receiving Transaction?


Easy Steps:

 

1. Click Canvass->Canvass Transaction

2. Select Reason

3. Search Item->Press Enter

4. Input Quantity->Press Enter

...repeat the process until all items entered

5. RightClick canvassed item->Click Canvass

6. DoubleClick selected supplier

...repeat the process until all canvassed items have suppliers

7. Click Save-F4

8. Type in Remarks

9. Click Save

 

10. Click Canvass->Canvass For Approval

11. Select Canvass Receipt

12. Right Click SupplierName->Click Approved

13. Click Approved button

 

14. Click Purchasing->PO Transaction

15. RightClick the EmptySpace on the right tab

16. Click Load Canvass

17. DoubleClick SupplierName

18. Click Save-F4

19. Select PaymentType

20. Click Save

 

21. Click Receiving->Receiving Transaction

22. RightClick the EmptySpace on the right tab

23. Click Load PO

24. DoubleClick selected PO

25. Click Save-F4

26. Select PaymentType

27. Click Save
