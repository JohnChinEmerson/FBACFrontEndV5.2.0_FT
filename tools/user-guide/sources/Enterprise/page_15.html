15. How to setup bank/cash accounts?


Easy Steps:

 

1. Click Cashflow->Cash Accounts

2. Click New

3. Select CashAccountType

4. Input Cash Account (for bank account bankname-accountno e.i. BDO-0876123245)

5. Click Save

 

6. Click Cashflow->Cash Transfers

7. Click New Cash Transfer

8. Select Source->Opening Balance

9. Select Destination

10. Input Amount

11. Click Save
