28. How to transact expense and pay with disbursed petty cash?


Easy Steps:

 

1. Click Expenses->Expense Transaction

2. Search Particular->Press Enter

3. Input Amount->Press Enter

...repeat the process until all particulars entered

4. Click Save-F4

5. Select PaymentType (except charge)

6. Click Save

 

7. Click Disbursement->Disbursement Requests

8. Click View Requests

9. Select Receipt

10. Click Action->Offset from the Disbursed Petty Cash

11. Select Petty Cash

12. Click Update
